Request data deletion

Bizgarh (Bizgarh Media Ventures Pvt. Ltd) — how to delete your account or personal data.

This page is for learners of the Bizgarh website and Android app on Google Play. You can request that we delete your account and/or associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to desk@bizgarh.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data deletion request.
  3. Include the email address you use to sign in to Bizgarh.
  4. Say whether you want:
    • Full account deletion (close the account and remove personal profile data), or
    • Partial data deletion (remove specific personal data while keeping the account, where legally allowed).
  5. We will confirm by email and process the request.

What we delete

When you request deletion (and where legally allowed), we delete or anonymise:

  • Account profile details (name, email, phone if provided)
  • Profile photo and edit-profile fields stored for your account
  • Support messages tied to your account where retention is not required

What we may keep

We may retain limited records when required by law or for legitimate business needs, for example:

  • Payment / purchase records and invoices (tax and accounting)
  • Fraud-prevention or security logs for a limited period
  • Information we must keep under applicable Indian or other laws

Course purchase history needed for financial compliance may be kept in anonymised or restricted form after account closure.

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your identity (the login email). Some backup systems may take longer to fully purge, up to 90 days.
